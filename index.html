Clipping Publisher

Clipping Publisher is a private, single-owner tool. Its owner uses it to upload short vertical videos (clips) that he edits himself to his own TikTok accounts, as drafts he then reviews and posts from the TikTok app.

The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up and is not used to access anyone else's account.

Not affiliated with TikTok, ByteDance, Instagram, Meta or any game publisher.
