Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Whose data

Clipping Publisher is used only by its owner, with TikTok accounts the owner controls. It does not collect data from other people, has no visitors' accounts and uses no cookies, analytics or advertising on this website.

2. What the tool stores

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens that TikTok issues when the owner authorizes the tool, used only to upload the owner's videos. They are stored in private, access-restricted storage controlled by the owner and are never shared.
  • Basic profile information returned by TikTok (open ID, display name) to know which of the owner's accounts a video is sent to.

3. What it does not do

It does not read messages, followers, viewing history or any other user data, does not sell or share data, and does not post publicly on its own: videos arrive as drafts in the TikTok inbox and the owner posts them in the app.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use. Revoking access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services / Manage app permissions) immediately stops the tool; the stored tokens are then deleted.
